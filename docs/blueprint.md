# **App Name**: RuralReach

## Core Features:

- Shop Owner Onboarding & Profile: Enable shop owners to register and manage their shop's profile, including business hours, contact information, service offerings, and location details.
- Dynamic Open/Closed Status: Allow shop owners to easily update their real-time open/closed status, providing immediate visibility to customers.
- Product/Service Listing Management: Shop owners can add, edit, and manage their inventory of products or services, including current stock levels and availability, leveraging a backend database.
- Local Browse & Search: Users can efficiently discover shops and services by browsing categories, using keywords, or filtering based on their proximity and requirements.
- Product Inquiry & Reservation: Customers can check the availability of specific items and send a reservation or inquiry request directly to a shop before commencing their travel.
- Direct Contact & Navigation: Users can view and directly call the shop via its listed contact number, and utilize integrated map functionality to get precise navigation to the shop's location.
- AI Product Description Tool: An AI-powered tool to assist shop owners in generating clear, concise, and engaging descriptions for their listed products or services with minimal effort.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Muted, natural green (#7FB496), selected for its association with growth, nature, and community, fostering a sense of calm and reliability. It complements a light background scheme without being overpowering.
- Background color: A very light, subtle green-tinted off-white (#ECF4EF). This choice provides a clean, open, and inviting canvas, allowing content to stand out while maintaining a natural and harmonious feel with the primary color.
- Accent color: A bolder, clear green (#449929). This color is chosen to create strong visual contrast for key interactive elements like buttons and calls to action, guiding the user's attention effectively.
- Main font: 'PT Sans' (humanist sans-serif). This font was selected for its excellent readability across different screen sizes, its modern yet warm character, and its versatility for both headlines and body text, making it suitable for community-focused content.
- Use clear, intuitive, and universally recognizable icons to represent actions and information such as shop open/closed status, location, contact, and product categories, ensuring ease of understanding for all users.
- Employ a card-based layout for displaying shop and product listings. This structure ensures content is organized, easily digestible, and scalable for both mobile-first responsiveness and larger screens, providing a clean user interface.
- Incorporate subtle and meaningful animations for interactions such as filtering results, expanding shop details, or form submissions, enhancing user feedback and contributing to a fluid and engaging user experience without distraction.
